Portions of the software for this repository make use of the pico-sdk (http://github.com/raspberrypi/pico-sdk/) from Raspberry Pi.
The Pico SDK is licensed under a BSD-3 license.

Also used are portions of the TinyUSB library (https://github.com/hathach/tinyusb) for USB device support for MIDI in.
TinyUSB is licensed under a MIT license.
